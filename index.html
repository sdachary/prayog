Why local-first matters

01

Your data, your device

Files never touch a server. WebRTC connects devices directly. The only thing that travels is the connection handshake.

02

Works offline

Once loaded, every tool runs without internet. Compress images, diff text, calculate loans on a plane or in a basement.

03

No accounts, ever

No signup, no email, no tracking. Open the page and start working. Close it and leave no trace.

04

Open source

Every line is on GitHub. Audit it, fork it, self-host it. No vendor lock-in, no rug pulls.
