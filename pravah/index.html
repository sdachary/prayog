      Won't connect, or no internet on this Wi-Fi?

      Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some routers isolate devices from each other ("AP isolation" / "client isolation") — turn that off, or use the offline option below.

      Offline option: on the laptop that holds the files, run the small server in this folder (needs only Python 3):

      1. Download serve.py
      2. python3 serve.py ~/Videos
      3. Open the address it prints on your phone. Seeking works, and the phone can upload files back to the laptop.
      Media goes directly between your devices over WebRTC — it is not uploaded anywhere.
      A public broker is used only to introduce the two devices (needs internet once, to pair).
